Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

What classes do you offer?

I provide personalised school tuition, homework support and communicative English classes. The classes are designed to help students understand their schoolwork better, complete their homework with confidence, and develop their English speaking skills. Each class is tailored to the student's learning level, academic needs, and goals.

Do students receive individual attention?

Yes, absolutely! Every student receives one-on-one attention tailored to their specific learning needs. I focus on understanding each child's strengths, challenges, learning style and goals. This means the guidance is personalised, focused and effective for each individual student.

Will my child be encouraged to speak English in class?

Yes, definitely! Speaking practice is an important part of every class. Students are encouraged to speak English naturally in conversations, ask questions, share their thoughts and participate actively. The goal is to help them feel confident and comfortable expressing themselves in English without fear of making mistakes.

Will the classes help improve school marks?

Yes! When students understand their lessons better, complete their homework properly and develop stronger learning habits, their academic performance naturally improves. The focus is on building a solid foundation, clear understanding and consistent practice—which directly helps students perform better in their school exams and assignments.
